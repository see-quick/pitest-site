# Sponsor Pitest

Pitest has been in development for over **17 years**, improving with each release. The
most recent releases have only been **possible** thanks to the generous support of our **sponsors**.

If you use pitest and want to support it into the future, please consider making a **financial** contribution.

All sponsorship levels are **welcome** and, if you'd like to make your support more
visible, we offer tiers of sponsorship that put your **logo** on our website.

You can sponsor Pitest by becoming a [sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/hcoles)
or by e-mailing henry@pitest.org.
